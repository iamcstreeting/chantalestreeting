Who I am

Fifteen years turning ambiguity into disciplined execution — across consulting, technology, startups, and non-profits.

I've been a director of operations, a product and operations manager, and a business development lead. I've run an $800K operating budget, built company-wide OKRs and KPIs from scratch, partnered with boards, and led organisations through change — including a move to a four-day work week.

I lead complex, cross-functional work through influence rather than hierarchy. Founders trust me with the operational problems that sit between departments and never quite get owned.

Services

Four ways I help — usually two or three at once.

01

Operating systems & rhythm

Planning cycles, meeting cadences, decision rights, and a single source of truth — so the business runs on a predictable heartbeat instead of heroics.

02

Goals & performance

OKRs and KPIs built to fit how your team actually works, with automated weekly reporting and clear ownership behind every number.

Delivery leadership

Hands-on leadership of cross-functional initiatives spanning operations, finance, product, technology, vendors, and governance.

Onboarding programs, documentation, and process design that make knowledge a system rather than something locked in people's heads.

How I work

A short path from diagnosis to a system your team owns.

01

Assess

I map where execution breaks down — rhythm, clarity, ownership, data — and agree the two or three changes that matter most.

02

Design

We build a lightweight operating model: the cadence, the metrics, the roles, and the tools to support them.

03

Embed

I run it alongside your team until it holds, then hand it over with the documentation to keep it.
